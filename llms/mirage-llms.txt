# Captions

> Captions is an AI video editor and generator built by Mirage (New York City). It transforms raw footage into fully-edited, stylized videos — or generates entirely new videos from a text prompt or selfie. Designed for content creators, influencers, small businesses, enterprise teams, marketers, agencies, and other professionals who need video to grow. Available on iOS, Android, and mobile web or desktop. Free to sign up; advanced features require a paid plan.

Captions automates the most time-consuming parts of video production: cutting scenes, adding B-roll, inserting music and sound effects, generating and styling captions, dubbing into 30+ languages, and correcting eye contact. Users can edit through a chat-based prompt interface, one-tap AI style presets, or manual timeline controls. Videos can be published directly to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and more.

## Features

- [Edit with AI](https://captions.ai/features/edit-with-ai): AI Edit applies styles, B-roll, transitions, music, and other elements to raw footage automatically. No editing skills required to edit an entire video at once. Includes a chat-based editor for prompt-driven changes, and quick fixes (trim filler words/pauses, denoise audio, adjust eye contact, remove backgrounds, add zooms, enhance footage).
- [Add Captions to Videos](https://captions.ai/features/add-captions-to-videos): Auto-transcription with 100+ stylizable caption templates. Customize font, color, size, animation, and placement. Supports 100+ languages.
- [Add Subtitles to Videos](https://captions.ai/features/add-subtitles-to-videos): Accurate subtitle generation synced to video timeline, with translation support for global audiences.
- [Translate Videos with AI](https://captions.ai/features/translate-videos-with-ai): Translate and dub videos into 30+ languages with a cloned version of the speaker's voice and lip movements synced to the new audio.
- [Generate AI Avatars](https://captions.ai/features/generate-ai-avatars): Create realistic AI video avatars from a photo, short clip, or text prompt. Includes a library of AI Actors and AI Twins (digital clones of the user). Customize outfits, backgrounds, and delivery. Avatars are generated as complete performances — audio, video, and visuals rendered together for natural results.
- [Correct Eye Contact](https://captions.ai/features/correct-your-eye-contact): AI adjusts a speaker's gaze to appear as direct eye contact with the camera, even if they were reading a teleprompter or looking away during recording.
- [AI B-Roll Generator](https://captions.ai/tools/ai-b-roll-generator): Automatically generates or suggests relevant B-roll footage to insert into videos.
